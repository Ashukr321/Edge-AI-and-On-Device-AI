# Local Speech Models — Wake word / keyword spotting

- **Porcupine** (Picovoice) — a commercial (free-tier available) cross-platform wake-word engine with a small footprint, custom wake-word training via their console, runs on microcontrollers up to phones.
- **openWakeWord** — an open-source, MIT-licensed wake-word framework using small ONNX models, trainable on synthetic TTS-generated data without needing recorded samples of the phrase.
- **TensorFlow Lite Micro keyword spotting** — reference examples (e.g. the "micro speech" example) for running KWS on microcontrollers with only kilobytes of RAM.
