# Local Speech Models — Overview

Speech models split into three distinct jobs: converting speech to text (ASR/STT), converting text to speech (TTS), and detecting a trigger phrase in a low-power always-listening loop (wake word / keyword spotting). Each has mature, efficient options that run comfortably on phones, laptops, and even microcontrollers, which is why "Hey Siri"-style assistants and offline dictation have been technically possible for years — the 2022-2024 shift was Whisper making high-accuracy *general-purpose* offline ASR available as an open model.
