# TinyML — Typical use cases

- **Keyword spotting / wake-word detection** — e.g. "Hey device", always-on audio classifiers under 20 KB.
- **Vibration / acoustic anomaly detection** — predictive maintenance on motors and industrial equipment using accelerometer or microphone data.
- **Gesture recognition** — IMU-based classifiers (accelerometer/gyroscope) for wearables and controllers.
- **Visual wake words / person detection** — tiny (~250 KB) CNNs distinguishing "person present" vs. not, at very low frame rates.
- **Environmental sensing** — classifying air quality, sound, or vibration patterns for battery-powered IoT sensors.
