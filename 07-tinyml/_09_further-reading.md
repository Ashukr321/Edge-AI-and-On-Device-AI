# TinyML — Further reading

- *TinyML* by Pete Warden and Daniel Situnayake (O'Reilly) — the foundational book on the subject, built around TensorFlow Lite Micro and the Arduino Nano 33 BLE Sense.
- TensorFlow Lite for Microcontrollers official documentation (tensorflow.org/lite/microcontrollers)
- Edge Impulse documentation and public projects (docs.edgeimpulse.com)
- Arm CMSIS-NN documentation (Arm Software developer site)
- The tinyML Foundation (tinyml.org) — conference talks, papers, and the broader community organization
- "Efficient Processing of Deep Neural Networks" by Sze, Chen, Yang, and Emer — background on hardware-aware efficient inference
