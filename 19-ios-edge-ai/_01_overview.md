# iOS Edge AI — Overview

Apple's on-device ML stack centers on **Core ML**, a unified inference framework that runs across CPU, GPU, and the Apple Neural Engine (ANE), plus higher-level frameworks (**Vision**, **Natural Language**, **Sound Analysis**) built on top of it. Starting with iOS 18/26-era releases, Apple also exposes its own on-device large language model through the **Foundation Models framework**, part of the "Apple Intelligence" initiative. This document covers the iOS/Apple-specific stack; cross-platform mobile concepts are in `03-mobile-ai`.
