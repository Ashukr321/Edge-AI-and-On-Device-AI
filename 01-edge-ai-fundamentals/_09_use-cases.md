# Edge AI Fundamentals — Use cases

- Computer vision: defect detection on production lines, retail shelf
  analytics, security/person detection, ADAS (lane/obstacle detection).
- Speech: on-device wake-word detection, offline transcription.
- Anomaly detection & predictive maintenance: vibration/acoustic sensors
  on industrial equipment flagging early failure signs without needing a
  cloud connection.
- Agriculture/environmental monitoring in low-connectivity areas.
