# On-Device AI — Why it matters

- **Zero network dependency**: features keep working in airplane mode,
  on the subway, or in a basement with no signal.
- **Instant responsiveness**: no server round trip means predictable,
  very low latency — critical for keyboard prediction, camera viewfinder
  effects, and AR.
- **Privacy by construction**: sensitive data (typed text, photos, voice,
  health signals) never leaves the device, which is a strong privacy and
  compliance story and is often explicitly marketed as such (e.g. Apple's
  on-device Siri requests, Google's on-device Gboard/Assistant features).
- **Cost at scale**: no per-inference cloud compute bill when you have
  hundreds of millions of devices doing inference constantly (e.g. every
  keystroke).
