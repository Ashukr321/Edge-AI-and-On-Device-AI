# On-Device AI — Overview

On-device AI is the subset of edge AI where inference runs **entirely
within the end-user's device** — phone, laptop, wearable, smart speaker,
car head unit — with no network round trip required for the model to
produce a result. This is a stricter definition than "edge": a factory
gateway server processing camera feeds is edge AI, but it is not on-device
AI from the perspective of the camera or the phone displaying the alert.
On-device AI specifically means "the computation happens on *this*
consumer/embedded device, right now, offline-capable."
