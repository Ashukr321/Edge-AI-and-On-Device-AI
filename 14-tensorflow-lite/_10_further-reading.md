# TensorFlow Lite / LiteRT — Further reading

- LiteRT documentation: https://ai.google.dev/edge/litert (converter guide, delegate guide, quantization guide).
- `ai-edge-torch` GitHub repository (Google AI Edge) for PyTorch → LiteRT conversion.
- TensorFlow Lite for Microcontrollers documentation, part of the LiteRT/AI Edge docs, and the *TinyML* book by Pete Warden and Daniel Situnayake for background on the microcontroller runtime's design constraints.
- MediaPipe Solutions / Tasks documentation for pre-built on-device pipelines.
- Netron: https://github.com/lutzroeder/netron
