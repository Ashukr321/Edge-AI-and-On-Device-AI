# Model Compression — Further reading

- "Deep Compression: Compressing Deep Neural Networks with Pruning, Trained Quantization and Huffman Coding" (Han, Mao, Dally, 2016)
- "The Lottery Ticket Hypothesis: Finding Sparse, Trainable Neural Networks" (Frankle & Carbin, 2019)
- "Distilling the Knowledge in a Neural Network" (Hinton, Vinyals, Dean, 2015) — the original knowledge distillation paper
- DistilBERT: "DistilBERT, a distilled version of BERT" (Sanh et al., Hugging Face)
- MobileBERT: "MobileBERT: a Compact Task-Agnostic BERT for Resource-Limited Devices" (Sun et al.)
- MobileNetV2/V3 and EfficientNet papers (Sandler et al.; Howard et al.; Tan & Le)
- TensorFlow Model Optimization Toolkit documentation (tensorflow.org/model_optimization)
- PyTorch pruning tutorial and `torch.nn.utils.prune` documentation (pytorch.org/tutorials)
- Hugging Face Optimum documentation (huggingface.co/docs/optimum)
