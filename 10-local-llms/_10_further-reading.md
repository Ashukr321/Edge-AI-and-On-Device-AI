# Local LLMs — Further reading

- llama.cpp repository and discussions (ggml-org/llama.cpp on GitHub) — quantization formats, benchmarks, backend support.
- Ollama documentation and model library.
- Hugging Face model cards for Llama 3.x, Mistral, Phi-4, Gemma 3, and Qwen2.5 — architecture details and license terms.
- r/LocalLLaMA community — active source of real-world hardware benchmarks and quantization comparisons.
- "GGUF" and "GGML" format specs in the llama.cpp repo's `docs/` and `gguf-py` package.
