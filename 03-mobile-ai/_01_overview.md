# Mobile AI — Overview

Mobile AI is the practice of running machine learning models directly on smartphones and tablets — inference (and sometimes on-device fine-tuning) happening on the device itself rather than in the cloud. It is the largest and most mature segment of edge AI: billions of iOS and Android devices ship every year with dedicated ML silicon, and both platform vendors treat on-device inference as a first-class capability.

This folder takes a cross-platform view. Platform-specific deep dives live in `18-android-edge-ai` and `19-ios-edge-ai`.
